01 / The study experience

Readings and practice
in one place.

AgingAndFamily combines course readings, translations, practice questions, and incorrect-answer review. I defined the screens and behaviors needed for studying.

02 / Changes I requested

Make the answer
easier to reach.

I requested the removal of extra controls from the reading screen, including font-size adjustment, bookmarks, and reading-position storage.

For the discussion-preparation view, I requested removing the input field so the first suggested answer appears directly below the question, while source evidence and follow-up questions remain expandable. The separate quiz input and grading features were kept.

03 / Project scope

Content with
source connections.

The site contains 22 readings, 417 pairs of original passages and translations, and 990 quiz questions.

AI coding tools implemented the changes, ran checks, and handled deployment. The image above shows a previously captured local page rather than a new verification of the live site.
